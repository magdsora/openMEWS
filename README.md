# OpenMEWS

A four-week-ahead forecast of the risk that a health district in the African meningitis belt crosses WHO's meningitis alert threshold, for more than 400 districts in five countries.

Live: https://magdsora.github.io/openmews/

Made by Magd Sora and Felipe Chen (team Shrimps of Jinchun) for the 2026 ACTION Hackathon. It received a top-10 Excellence Award at the final, held at the University of Hong Kong in September 2026.

Sources: weekly meningitis surveillance from national ministries of health, via WHO; ERA5-Land and CAMS reanalysis (Copernicus); district outlines from UN OCHA.
